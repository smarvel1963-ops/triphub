Trip Hub Pass

Trip Hub is free for planning your trips: bookings, map, leave times, splitting the cost, packing and diary. The Trip Hub Pass is $9.99 a year, for as many trips as you like that year. Your data

Your trip information lives on your phone, and in your own Google Drive if you turn on backup. You are responsible for keeping a backup. Phones get lost and browsers get cleared, and we cannot recover data we never had.

Check your times, documents and bookings

Trip Hub keeps what you type in, and what it works out from it (leave times, countdowns, packing and document lists) is a guide. Always check flight, hotel, rental car and ticket times, your passport, visa and other travel documents, and any booking details with the airline, hotel or company itself. Maps, weather and place information can be wrong or out of date. Don't rely on Trip Hub alone for a connection or a deadline. AI helper answers can be wrong, so check anything important.
